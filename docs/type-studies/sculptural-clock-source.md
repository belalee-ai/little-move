## 生成提示词

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a SINGLE photographic cutout asset for an animated sculptural pendulum clock, isolated with true transparent background. Portrait composition, about 2:3 aspect. Primary request: sophisticated contemporary kinetic wall sculpture, original design rather than a branded replica. A small dark bronze cylindrical clock hub at horizontal center around 23 percent image height, with only two very slender sculpted walnut wood clock hands pointing to approximately 10:10, spanning the upper third. No face, no dial ring, no numbers, no enclosing clock case. From directly underneath the hub a perfectly straight very thin bronze pendulum rod extends vertically downward on the image centerline, ending in a large elegant satin bronze circular disk at around 83 percent image height. The rod must be straight vertical and unobstructed, with ample clear rod segment separating the hub and disk so it can be animated as a separate lower piece. The disk is front-facing with a slight physical thickness visible at its right edge, subtle concentric brushed metal texture and a beautiful long warm specular reflection. Photoreal macro product photography, refined museum collectible, tactile walnut grain, real machined metal microtexture, controlled studio side light from upper left, softly shaded volume, crisp silhouette, believable lens and material response. Entire object visible with 7 percent edge margin. NO floor or wall, NO cast shadow on any background, NO text, NO letters, NO branding, NO watermarks, NO graphic illustration or vector style. This will be composited on a warm limestone wall with dark brown text, so materials should be medium-dark walnut and muted bronze, no shiny yellow gold.
